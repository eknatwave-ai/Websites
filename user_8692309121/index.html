### Slingshot Physics & Turn-Based Play The game uses a custom 2D physics engine to bring block-stacking destruction to life. - **Slingshot controls:** Drag back from the slingshot and release to launch a bird. A dotted trajectory preview shows the predicted arc before you let go. - **Bird abilities:** Tap during flight to trigger a special move. Yellow accelerates, black explodes with a wide radius, and red has no active ability but is balanced for direct impact. - **Damage model:** Impacts above a speed threshold deal damage to blocks and pigs. Ice breaks easily, stone requires heavier hits, and TNT explodes on destruction, damaging nearby objects. - **Progression:** Five levels come with pre-built structures. Clear all pigs to advance, or lose if you run out of birds. Score is saved locally as a best record. --- **Optimization Tip:** You can tweak the `POWER` constant to change launch strength, or adjust `GRAV` for a floatier or heavier feel. To add levels, append new entries to the `LEVELS` array with matching block and pig coordinates.
