### Gameplay and Design The game recreates the classic slingshot physics with a few extra twists. - **Launch & Aim:** Drag back from the slingshot to set the launch angle and power, then release. A dotted trajectory preview helps you line up shots. - **Bird Abilities:** While a bird is in flight, tap anywhere to activate its unique skill: the yellow bird speeds up, the blue bird splits into three, the black bird explodes, the green bird reverses direction, and the white bird drops an explosive egg. - **Materials & Destruction:** Blocks are made of wood, ice, stone, glass, or TNT. Each has different durability and reacts to impacts. Destroy TNT for a chain explosion. - **Level Progression & Scoring:** Clear all pigs to finish a level. Points come from destroying blocks and defeating pigs, with a bonus for each unused bird. Your best score is saved locally. - **Controls & Feedback:** Use the on-screen buttons to pause, restart, or toggle sound. Visual effects like screen shake and particle bursts confirm hits. This keeps the core loop simple while adding enough variety to each shot. --- **Optimization Tip:** You can adjust the gravity constant `G` or the number of birds and level layouts in the `LEVELS` array. For example, increasing `G` makes the game feel faster and more difficult, while editing the `birds` arrays changes which abilities appear on each level.
