### Gameplay Mechanics & Neon Feedback Here's how the game plays and how you can control it. - **Objective & progression:** Clear all neon bricks to advance. There are 12 levels with increasing brick rows, health, and speed; every 3rd level grants an extra life. - **Controls:** Move the paddle with ←/→ or A/D, or by moving your mouse/finger. Press Space or tap to launch the ball. P pauses, M toggles sound. - **Power-ups:** Dropped bricks may release wide paddle, multi-ball, slow motion, laser, or extra life pickups. Active effects show in the HUD. - **Visual & audio feedback:** Neon glow, particle bursts, screen shake, and synthesized retro sounds give immediate response to every hit, break, and power-up. --- **Optimization Tip:** You can adjust difficulty by changing `S.lives` (starting lives), `S.maxLevel` (total levels), the ball speed formula in `ballSpeed()`, and the power-up drop chance (`0.16`) inside `damageBrick()`.
