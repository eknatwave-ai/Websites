### Storefront Mechanics & Purchasing Flow Here’s a quick walkthrough of the main parts that make shopping straightforward. - **Age verification gate** – A full-screen 18+ check blocks access until confirmed. The choice is stored in localStorage so returning visitors skip it. - **Product catalog** – Sixteen cigarette items are filterable by category (classic, slim, superslim, menthol, capsule) and searchable by brand or name. Each card shows nicotine and tar specs, price, and an add-to-cart button. - **Cart drawer** – A slide-out panel lists items, lets you adjust quantities or remove products, and automatically calculates delivery fees. Free shipping kicks in at 3,000 ₽. - **Checkout modal** – A simple form collects name, phone, delivery method, address, and comments. Basic validation warns if the name is too short or the phone is incomplete, then shows a success confirmation. - **Toast notifications** – Small pop-up messages confirm when items are added or removed from the cart. --- **Optimization Tip:** You can easily update the product list by editing the `PRODUCTS` array in the script. Each entry includes price, category, and `pack` colors that control the pack gradient. The minimum order and free-shipping threshold are set by `MIN_ORDER` and `SHIP_FREE_FROM` variables at the top of the script.
