### Gameplay Mechanics & Interaction This neon arcade shooter focuses on fast, responsive action with clear visual feedback for every hit. - **Movement & Shooting:** Control your ship with WASD, arrow keys, or by dragging with the mouse or finger. Your ship fires automatically, so you can concentrate on dodging enemy fire and collecting power-ups. - **Enemy Types & Waves:** Each wave introduces a mix of grunts (swaying side-to-side), divers (homing in on you), and shooters (firing aimed projectiles). Every fifth wave spawns a boss with a wide spread attack and a health bar at the top. - **Power-Ups:** Destroyed enemies may drop pickups that grant a shield (absorbs one hit), a triple-shot upgrade, or a heart to restore a life. You can collect them by flying over them. - **Real-Time Feedback:** Hits produce particle bursts, screen shake, and synthesized sound effects. A red flash appears when you take damage, and your ship briefly blinks during invulnerability. The HUD shows your score, current wave, and remaining lives. - **Pause & High Score:** Press P or Escape to pause. Your best score is saved locally and shown on the menu and game-over screens. --- **Optimization Tip:** You can adjust the default starting lives by changing the `lives` variable from `3` to another value near the top of the script. The wave delay is controlled by `waveTimer` (default `3.0`), and enemy spawn pace depends on the `gap` calculation inside `nextWave()`.
