### Gameplay & Interaction Essentials The game runs entirely in your browser, with keyboard, mouse, and touch controls all supported. - **Auto-fire combat** – Your ship shoots continuously; you only need to move and dodge. The fire rate increases with the rapid power-up. - **Wave progression** – Each wave spawns a mix of grunts, darters, shooters, and tanks. Every fifth wave introduces a boss with a health bar at the top. - **Power-ups** – Defeated enemies drop floating pickups: triple shot, rapid fire, temporary shield, and extra lives. Active effects appear in the HUD. - **Controls** – WASD or arrow keys to move; drag on screen or use the mouse to follow. Press P or Escape to pause, and M to toggle sound. - **On-screen HUD** – Score, wave number, remaining lives, and active buffs are displayed in the top corners without interrupting play. --- **Optimization Tip:** You can adjust difficulty by modifying values inside `resetGame()` and `startWave()` — for example, `state.lives`, enemy `speed`, or the power-up drop chance (currently `0.11`). The starting player speed is `440` in `resetGame()`.
