### Gameplay and Interaction Overview You control a neon ship, dodge enemy fire, and collect power-ups to survive as long as possible. - **Controls:** Use the left and right arrow keys or A/D to move. Press Space to shoot. On touch screens, drag your finger across the screen to steer. - **Enemies and difficulty:** Three enemy types appear: fast diagonal movers, hovering shooters, and slow tanky enemies. The spawn rate increases over time, making each run progressively harder. - **Power-ups:** Dropped by destroyed enemies, these grant rapid fire, triple shots, a temporary shield, or an extra life. Active effects appear in the bottom-left corner. - **Score and lives:** Destroying enemies adds points, with an extra bonus for collecting power-ups. You start with three lives, and the best score is saved locally in your browser. - **Visual feedback:** Screen shake, particle bursts, and enemy flash effects make hits and explosions feel responsive, while a starfield background adds depth to the neon aesthetic. --- **Optimization Tip:** You can adjust the base movement speed (`player.speed` near the top of the script) or the fire cooldown values (`0.148` and `0.082`) to change difficulty. Power-up strengths and spawn timers are also stored in the same region, so you can tweak them without touching the rendering logic.
